## Ranked proposals

### A. Honesty about what it knows (the 25 Nov class of failure)
1. **Provenance on every fact.** `{source: sentence, at: time, via: said|noted|guessed|from a file}` on people/relations/tastes/terms/facts in
   `knowledge.json` and entity attributes. "Where did you get that?" reads it; the wondering pass may only store `via: said`. A fact with
   `via: guessed` is shown as a guess ("I think ...") and asked about.
2. **Correction by rule**: "my brother is not Omar", "that's not true", "that's wrong, Linda is my cousin" -> remove or replace the fact, say
   what changed, add a line to `state/failures.jsonl`. (Today only "forget X" exists.)
3. **"How do you work?" answers** from `documentation/` (the Mind page text and `manual.py`): "how do you use your emotions", "how do you
   remember", "what happens when you sleep", "what is a concept", "what is repair mode".
4. **A turn history**: the last 10 answers with how each was made (rule / tool / model). "What did you say before that", "say that
   again", "what was the answer before", "what did I ask before that".

### B. Emotions that show (they exist; connect them)
5. **The voice follows the state**: the speak actuator's speed and pause from the emotion engine's energy/patience (slower and flatter when
   low, brisker when keen); shorter answers when patience is low. (`modulation.energy`/`verbosity` are in the prompt only today.)
6. **The face follows the person**: a concerned face when repair mode starts or the person's mood trend is low; a small smile when they thank
   it; back to idle when they are fine. Uses `face.on_feeling`; no new animation needed.
7. **A mood line in the briefings**: the morning/evening briefing says how the last day went from the stored episode moods ("a harder day:
   40% of what you said sounded low, mostly around 15:00-16:00 -- NoSQL") and the weekly review compares weeks.
8. **Repair mode on the Mind page**: how often it triggered, the lines that led to it, and what ASH answered -- the list of failures
   as a list.

### C. For a student (what this machine is used for)
9. **Quiz me from my notes**: "quiz me on NoSQL chapter 5" -> questions built from the text of that Samsung note (the reader exists), asked
   and marked by `games.py`'s quiz; wrong answers are kept for the next time ("you keep missing document stores").
10. **Class-aware notes**: at class time offer "new note for NoSQL?" (Samsung Notes writer + timetable); after class "anything to add?";
    "what did I write about X last week" with dates.
11. **"What's due" / deadlines** from what you say ("the report is due Thursday") into reminders with the class: the reminder rule
    plus the timetable; said in the morning briefing.
12. **Study sessions**: "start a 45-minute session on NoSQL" -> focus mode (exists) + the Samsung note open + a break reminder + a line at
    the end from the activity record ("45 minutes, 3 windows").

### D. The developer's loop (you are the developer)
13. **A daily failure digest**: `state/failures.jsonl` + `state/unrouted.jsonl` grouped by meaning, with a suggested rule and a one-line
    test for each, written to `documentation/digest-YYYY-MM-DD.md` at the nightly sleep. The 25 Nov session would have been a file you
    could hand over, not an afternoon.
14. **A thumbs-down in the console**: one click tags the answer "wrong", stores the question, the answer and how it was made, and counts
    as a repair signal.
15. **Replay fixtures**: `tools/fixtures_from_failures.py` turns those lines into the `check(...)` cases the tests use.

### E. The machine
16. **Lazy-load the heavy models** (OCR, vision, classifier, voiceprint): listening in ~15 s, the rest on first use. The footprint line in
    the console ("ASH 1.8 GB, qwen2.5:3b loaded") from the governor's numbers.
17. **A small-talk model** (a ~1.5B model for greetings, thanks and "how are you": ~2 s) -- `modelpick.py` routes it once installed.
18. **Name cleanup**: when "Your name" changes, rewrite the old "Immortal:" headings in episodes/notes (a backed-up migration).

### F. Connect what is already there

20. **Reminders + quiet + the repair state**: reminders that come due while muted or while the person is frustrated wait, then are said in order.
21. **Calendar**: still needs a feed link (`calendar.urls`); then the morning briefing and "what did I miss" use it.
22. **Screen + notes**: "save this to a note" -- the text of the window in front (the screen reader) into a new Samsung note, read back first.
23. **Files + media**: "what am I watching" from the window title, "who is this" from the page title, nothing leaves the machine.
