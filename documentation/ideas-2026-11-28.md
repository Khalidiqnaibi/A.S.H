## Ranked proposals

### A. Say it once, do both (compound and context)
1. **Compound commands.** `compound.py`: split "X and Y" / "X then Y" where both halves parse as a known feature (desktop, control, media, reminders, notes);
   run in order; stop at the first that fails and say which. The pipe layer (`pipeline.py`) already chains typed stages; this is its voice front door for
   two short imperatives. Test: "switch to Opera and make it full screen" -> Opera in front, `f11`/`f`, one spoken line.
2. **Taught rules that act.** The habit store (28 Nov) is only shown to the model. When a habit's trigger is the whole phrase ("see how something works"),
   run its meaning through the matching feature ("watch a YouTube video about it" -> `websearch` on YouTube for the current topic). `habits.apply(text)` before the model.
3. **Click with confirmation.** In `control.find_text`, if two matches are close in score, highlight neither but ask "the one at the top or the bottom?"; remember the choice
   as a skill for that window title ("on this YouTube page, 'Subscribe' is at the right").
4. **"Do it" after learning.** `learnweb` saves a concept and a skill; "from Google, learn how to open Discord and type in it" followed by "now do it" should run the saved
   steps with `control` (keys/click/type), asking yes first. Connects learnweb, skills, control.

### B. Honesty that helps, not just says "I don't know"
5. **"I don't know" offers to learn.** After "I don't know that one" (or the model's), ASH offers once: "Want me to look it up and keep it?" A yes goes to `learnweb`
   (offline: queued). Turns the worst moment of the 28 Nov morning into the feature that was being tested.
6. **Check yourself.** "Is that right?", "do you think that's correct", "that's not correct" re-run the last answer's source and compare:
   rule/tool answers re-run; note/memory answers show the note and its date; model answers say "that came from the model, I can't check it -- want me to look it up?".
7. **Prompt-size and speed on the console.** The 28 Nov change logs prompt characters per turn. Show the last ten turns on the Mind page (route, seconds, prompt size)
   so "why was that slow" is a number. Also stream the first sentence of a model answer to speech while the rest is still generating (the speak actuator already works by sentence).
8. **"Still thinking (12 s) -- say stop".** After 8 s of a model turn, one short cue with the cancel word, so you cancel on purpose rather than by "Nevermind".

### C. The machine and the room
9. **Can my PC run it.** `canrun.py`: a small offline table (a few hundred titles/apps with min and recommended CPU/RAM/VRAM) plus the self-model's real numbers;
   "can this run GTA 6" -> "not on 4 GB of video memory: it asks for 8; it would run Minecraft and Valorant". Extends `selfmodel` and `inventory`.
10. **Room filter and hygiene.** Drop, before journal and memory, lines that were not addressed and are not commands (TV, game audio, "don't forget to hit that button"),
    and any line with slurs or abuse (not stored, not answered, not learned). `hygiene.py` already cleans memory afterwards; this stops it at the door.
11. **Facts about the person.** "My birthday is 3 May", "I study computer engineering at ...", "I'm 21": a profile in the notes, answered by rule ("How old am I", "how old is Immortal" once merged),
    and used in the morning briefing (birthdays, exam dates).
12. **A name that isn't an app.** "Switch to Claude" with no such app: suggest the closest windows/tabs from the tabs sensor and the inventory instead of 68 s of model.
